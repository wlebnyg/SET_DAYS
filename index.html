About

SET Days 2026 is a three-day event organized by the School of Engineering and Technology at NU Fairview, taking place on December 2-4, 2026. The event aims to bring students together through activities that highlight technology, crativity, innovation, and the importance of continuous learning in the modern world.

Throughout the event, students will have the opportunity to explore different technology-related activities, paticipate in proposed competitions, and discover new ideas. These activities are designed to encourage collaboration, develop practical skills, and provide an enjoyable learning experience beyond the classroom.

SET Days 2026 welcomes students and faculty from the School of Engineering and Technology who are interested in exploring the latest trends, sharing their knowledge, and developing their abilities. Through this event, students are enrouraged to connect with one another, showcase their creativity, and become more inspired to contribute to the fast changing world of engineering and technology.

Technology Terms

E-Sports Tournament
A competitive gaming event where participants play video games against each other.

RJ45 Cable Making
A practical activity where participants learn to create Ethernet cables using RJ45 connectors.

An exhibition showcasing innovative technology projects and ideas from students and faculty.
